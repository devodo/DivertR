Plastic Is Pollution.
It's time our government treated it that way.

Every year, millions of tonnes of plastic end up in our rivers, oceans and countryside. PIP is a petition calling on the government to take plastic pollution seriously — with real targets, real regulation and real action.

Why it matters

400M+

Tonnes of plastic a year

Global plastic production keeps rising every year — and most of it is used once, then thrown away.

8M

Tonnes into our oceans

An estimated eight million tonnes of plastic waste enters the ocean annually, harming wildlife and ecosystems.

450 yrs

To break down

A single plastic bottle can take centuries to degrade — and even then it only breaks into microplastics.

What your signature does

Every signature strengthens our case when we present this petition to government. We're asking for legally binding reduction targets for single-use plastic, proper investment in recycling infrastructure, and accountability for the biggest plastic polluters.

It takes less than a minute to add your name — and every name counts.
