What We're Doing

The petition is just one part of the campaign. Here's what we're working on right now, what's coming next, and what we've already done.

Happening now

Happening now

Collecting signatures

Our main focus is growing the petition. We're sharing it in schools, community groups and online — every signature makes our voice louder.

Happening now

Awareness campaign

We're producing posters, social media posts and short talks explaining why plastic is pollution — not just litter — and what everyone can do about it.

Happening now

Community clean-ups

We run regular local litter picks. Every clean-up is logged, and the plastic we collect becomes evidence for the petition.

Coming up

Coming up

Presenting the petition

Once we reach our signature target, we'll formally deliver the petition document to our local MP and ask them to raise it in Parliament.

Coming up

Working with local schools

We're planning assemblies and workshops so young people can learn about plastic pollution and add their voices to the campaign.

Completed

Completed

Launched the petition

We launched PIP and this website to start collecting signatures and spreading the word.

How you can help

Sign the petition, share it with friends and family, and join us at a clean-up. If you'd like to volunteer or help organise, get in touch — we'd love to have you.
